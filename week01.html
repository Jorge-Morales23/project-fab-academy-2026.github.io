Introduction

This assignment introduces the foundation of Fab Academy. During this first week, we began to understand how the program works, the real importance of documenting every week, and the continuous record of our learning process. This project focuses on creating and finding new ways to innovate and build real solutions to the problems we identified throughout this process.

1. The Beginning

Think to Create

Every solution starts with a problem worth solving. Water is one of the most valuable resources in agriculture, yet a large part of it is wasted through inefficient irrigation, evaporation, and watering without real knowledge of what the plants actually need.

At the same time, growing our own food is becoming more important than ever. Self-sufficiency in agriculture means producing more with less: less water, less space, and less dependence on outside supplies. This is the need that guides our first ideas, and the starting point for everything we will design and build in the coming weeks.
