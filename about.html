About Our Group

General information about our group, our members, and our work in Fab Academy 2026.

Who We Are

We are four sixth-semester Industrial Engineering students at the Universidad de Cuenca, working together with the Industrial FabLab Ucuenca in Fab Academy 2026. Each of us brings different interests and skills to the team, and this page introduces every member and the work we have done so far.

This website documents the process we went through to create our Fab Academy 2026 project. Each week, we record and share what we learned along the way, including digital design, electronics, programming, machine design, and the development of our final project.

Our Team

Meet the four members of our team: who we are, what we contribute to the project, and what we enjoy outside of class.

What We Document

Every week we record the process behind our work. Our documentation covers:

  • Digital design (CAD, 2D and 3D modeling)
  • Electronics
  • Programming
  • Machine design
  • The development of our final project
